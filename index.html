04 / research

Research & Publications

Earlier applied-AI work from my engineering degree: a computer-vision safety system, the conference paper it led to, and a registered dataset.

Project

Vigilante: AI-based Real-Time Driver Safety Monitoring System

A webcam system that watches a driver's face and sounds an alarm when it spots drowsiness or yawning. Eye closure is measured with the eye aspect ratio from 68-point facial landmarks and turned into a drowsiness level with fuzzy logic, while yawns are caught by tracking mouth width. The alarm fires after about two seconds of closed eyes, and it keeps working with glasses and in low light.

I also built a face-recognition module that logs who is on camera, with a timestamp, to an Excel file.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Dlib
  • SciPy
  • Fuzzy logic
